# The Impact of E-Banking Service Quality on E-Customer Satisfaction

### Quantitative Research Proposal | Banking & Financial Services | Customer Satisfaction

## Project Overview

This research proposal examines the relationship between electronic banking service quality and customer satisfaction among undergraduate students at the University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka.

The proposed study focuses on how different dimensions of e-banking service quality influence students' satisfaction with online banking platforms.

It adopts a quantitative research approach using survey questionnaires and planned statistical analysis.

## Research Objectives

- Examine the relationship between e-banking service quality and e-customer satisfaction.
- Assess the role of reliability in customer satisfaction.
- Investigate how responsiveness affects customer experience.
- Evaluate the influence of security and privacy.
- Examine the relationship between web design, functionality, and customer satisfaction.

## Key Research Variables

**Dependent Variable:**
- E-Customer Satisfaction

**Independent Variables:**
- Reliability
- Responsiveness
- Security and Privacy
- Web Design
- Functionality

## Proposed Methodology

**Research Approach:** Quantitative

**Research Paradigm:** Positivism

**Target Population:** Undergraduate students at the University of Ruhuna

**Data Collection:** Structured questionnaire survey

**Planned Statistical Techniques:**
- Descriptive Statistics
- Correlation Analysis
- Multiple Linear Regression

**Proposed Software:** SPSS

## Expected Outcomes

The proposed research aims to identify service quality dimensions associated with undergraduate students' satisfaction with e-banking services.

The findings are expected to provide insights into how financial institutions can improve the reliability, security, responsiveness, and usability of their digital banking platforms.

*Note: This repository contains a research proposal. The expected outcomes are hypotheses and research expectations, not completed empirical findings.*
